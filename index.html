Curatorial Sources & Archival Credits

Archaeological Citations, Museum Collections, and Art-Historical Corpus

Major Institutional Repositories

  • National Museum, New Delhi: Harappan seals, Bronze Dancing Girl, Kushan-Gupta sculptures.
  • Archaeological Survey of India (ASI): Sanchi Stupa, Mamallapuram monuments, Konark Sun Temple, Sarnath Museum.
  • Government Museum, Chennai: Imperial Chola bronze masterpieces, Amaravati Buddhist reliefs.
  • Los Angeles County Museum of Art (LACMA) & British Museum: Verified open-access archival photography for Chola Nataraja and South Asian bronzes.

Essential Art-Historical Bibliography

  • Coomaraswamy, Ananda K. The Dance of Shiva: Essays on Indian Art and Culture. Munshiram Manoharlal.
  • Harle, J. C. The Art and Architecture of the Indian Subcontinent. Yale University Press Pelican History of Art.
  • Kramrisch, Stella. The Hindu Temple (Vols. I & II). Motilal Banarsidass.
  • Sivaramamurti, C. South Indian Bronzes. Lalit Kala Akademi, New Delhi.
  • Michell, George. The Hindu Temple: An Introduction to its Meaning and Forms. University of Chicago Press.
